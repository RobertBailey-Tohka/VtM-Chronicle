Project Nightwatch (1962)

During the height of the Cuban Missile Crisis, Army intelligence established an underground sub-basement below the gold bullion vaults designated "Project Nightwatch." Its mission was not anti-Soviet defense, but cataloging nocturnal human exsanguinations across the Ohio Valley and developing silver-nitrate ordnance to neutralize predatory humanoids.

The Blue Flames of 1974
Local History
Learned via: Library Microfiche / Interview with Elderly Resident Hidden
Official town records state the Old Grain Mill burned down due to faulty industrial wiring on Halloween night 1974. However, unreleased firefighter logs describe flames that burned with an unnatural blue-black hue and heat that warped steel girders in under five minutes. Several witnesses reported seeing three silhouetted figures sprinting away on foot toward Fort Knox at speeds exceeding sixty miles per hour.
The Abandonment of St. Christopher Parish
Kindred Secret
Learned via: Confession from Father Callahan / Church Basement Archives Hidden
In the winter of 1984, the Catholic diocese abruptly bolted the doors of St. Christopher Parish and reassigned the resident clergy overnight without explanation. While mortal rumors whispered of financial scandal, local Kindred know that an elder Lasombra conducted midnight confessionals in total darkness, granting mortals twisted absolution in exchange for their blood.
